Summary

Of every 100 retrievals, about 36 end in a baby. About 34 end in dropout: 19 of them did nothing more after the retrieval, and 15 came back for one or more transfers and then stopped coming. The other 31 continue with another retrieval or a donor-egg cycle, or cannot be evaluated yet.

Definitions in plain words

Definition version 0.10. The full rules are in Help and definition.

Dropout per cycle (primary measure). Each retrieval opens an attempt that ends in a baby (success), in dropout (12 months pass with no retrieval, transfer or donor-egg cycle after its last procedure, or there is a gap of more than 12 months inside the cycle) or continues (a new retrieval or donor-egg cycle). Transfers inside the cycle are neutral. A patient with more than one retrieval contributes more than one cycle. The primary rate is over the cycles that can already be evaluated.

Dropout per patient (secondary measure). Has the patient ever had 12 months with no procedure? Each patient counts once, at the first such stretch, even if she comes back later; it grows with follow-up.

Permanent dropout (secondary measure). A dropout after the patient's last event, with nothing afterwards. Shown by pathway step.

No return after the retrieval. The patient did not come back for anything in the 12 months after that retrieval. It is the measure Inception, Shen and CEGYR used so far; it is not dropout.

Dropout types G1, G2a, G2b and G3. G1: the retrieval left no viable embryo (no oocytes, no blastocyst or all aneuploid). G2a: embryos were left and there was no transfer in the cycle. G2b: after unsuccessful transfers, embryos remain. G3: after unsuccessful transfers, none remain. If embryos left are missing, G2b/G3 is shown together.

What it does not mean. It does not say the patient gave up treatment for good: she may continue at another clinic. The reason is not part of the definition.

Only the preventable part is recoverable; part of the dropout is an informed decision. Starting values come from the package (retrievals per year, per patient, dropout and returns) and can be changed.

What is not in the data, and methods

  • Continuity is measured per clinic: a patient who continues at another clinic shows as dropout.
  • No washout period: the first observed retrieval may not be the patient's first and her earlier history is not visible.
  • Transfers without a recorded outcome are treated as unsuccessful; the sensitivity analysis shows how much it changes.
  • Without the viable-embryo item, dropout without a transfer counts as G2a; without embryos left, as G2b/G3.
  • Cells from 1 to 10 suppressed ("<11") and whole rows when the base is under 11. No complementary suppression.
  • The text describes; it does not explain why. The reason for stopping is not part of the definition.
  • Methods: 95% Wilson confidence intervals. 12-month threshold provisional; DuoStim at 60 days; undated loss at 60 days after the transfer.
